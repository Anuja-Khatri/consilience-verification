# WP1 annotation guide (codebook v2) — designed-and-unrun

Repackaged from the prior Task-4 materials. A trained annotator applying the published
codebook (never a domain expert). Label each claim's (entity_type, axis, polarity) from
the quoted passage alone.

## entity_type
- **boundary**: A level-of-description / scale boundary claim.
- **constraint**: A restriction on what counts as/for consciousness (exclusion, function-insufficiency).
- **emergent_property**: A property that emerges (graded experience, machine consciousness).
- **field**: A field- or spacetime-level commitment (proto-experience in geometry).
- **information**: A quantity/measure claim (e.g., integrated information Phi).
- **invariant**: An identity claim: X IS Y, offered as identity not correlation.
- **measurement**: A predicted observable / empirical commitment.
- **observer**: A methodological commitment about admissible evidence (e.g., no-report paradigms).
- **process**: A dynamic identity: consciousness IS a process (global broadcast, reduction event).
- **state**: A temporal/dynamic claim about conscious content over time.
- **substrate**: A commitment about physical substrate dependence or independence.
- **system**: An architectural or locus claim about the conscious system.
- **threshold**: A quantitative threshold gating a unit event (E = h-bar/t).
- **transformation**: A mechanism that transforms state (ignition, objective reduction/collapse).

## axis
- **architecture**: required system architecture
- **computability**: computable/simulable vs non-computable
- **connectivity**: short-range posterior vs long-range fronto-posterior
- **fundamentality**: emergent vs fundamental/proto-experiential
- **identity_relation**: is-identity vs correlation
- **locus**: where conscious content lives (posterior vs PFC)
- **mechanism**: the causal mechanism invoked
- **mechanism_scale**: network scale vs molecular/quantum scale
- **observable_commitment**: the empirical/observable space committed to
- **substrate_dependence**: substrate-dependent vs substrate-independent
- **temporal_profile**: sustained vs phasic/ignition
- **unit_threshold**: what gates one discrete conscious unit/event
- **none**: the claim commits to no axis in the controlled list.

## polarity
- **asserts** / **denies** (hedged assertions have NO polarity value —
  log as a vocabulary limitation, not a label; this is the B2 hedging edge rule).

## worked examples
- 'Consciousness IS the posterior hot zone' → entity=locus-claim, axis=locus,
  value=posterior_hot_zone, polarity=asserts.
- 'X does NOT require a global workspace' →
  polarity=denies on the connectivity axis (negation flips polarity).
